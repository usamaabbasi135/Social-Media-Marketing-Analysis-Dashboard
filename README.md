# Social Media Marketing Analysis

This project involved a comprehensive analysis of social media marketing campaigns using Excel. The goal was to analyze, clean, and interpret data from various campaigns to derive actionable insights and visualize the results effectively. Below are the key highlights of the project:

## Data Cleaning
- Cleaned and standardized campaign data, including campaign goals, acquisition costs, and ROI metrics, ensuring consistency and accuracy.
- Leveraged Excel functions like `SUBSTITUTE`, `TEXT`, and `TRIM` to remove unwanted characters and format columns appropriately.

## Data Analysis
- Created pivot tables and charts to answer specific business questions, such as:
  - Which channels generated the highest ROI and net profit?
  - The engagement score across different customer segments.
  - A bar chart to visualize net profit by channel.

## Key Metrics Calculated
- ROI, Net Profit, and Engagement Score for each campaign.
- Click-through and impression-based performance metrics.

## Dashboard Creation
- Designed a visually interactive dashboard showcasing key performance indicators (KPIs) for decision-making.
- Included visualizations such as bar charts and line graphs to track campaign success over time and across channels.

## Tools and Techniques
- **Tools Used**: Microsoft Excel (Advanced Formulas, Pivot Tables, and Data Visualization).
- **Techniques**: Data Cleaning, Data Transformation, and Performance Analysis.

## Impact
This analysis enabled a deeper understanding of campaign performance across channels like Facebook, Twitter, and Pinterest, allowing marketing teams to focus on high-performing strategies and optimize budgets effectively.
